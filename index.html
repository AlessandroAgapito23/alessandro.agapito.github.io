Alessandro Agapito

Gravitational-wave cosmology

I build Bayesian methods that turn gravitational-wave detections into measurements of the expansion of the Universe, of the compact-binary population, and of the galaxies that host these mergers. I work with LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA data today and prepare the science case of the Einstein Telescope.

My Ph.D., supervised by Michele Mancarella at the Centre de Physique Théorique in Marseille, targets the systematics that limit standard-siren methods: the mass model and its redshift evolution, and the galaxy–merger relation, with methods designed to scale from the current network to third-generation detectors.
