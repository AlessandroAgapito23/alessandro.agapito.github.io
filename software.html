Software

Software

Code I use and develop. My own repositories are on GitHub.

§ Codes

  • pymcpop-gw

    Hierarchical population and cosmology inference with HMC in PyMC. The dark-siren host-ranking scheme is built on it.

  • icarogw

    LVK collaboration pipeline that I run on GWTC-6 data for the cosmology paper expected in December 2026.

  • chimera

    Spectral- and dark-siren pipeline, one of those stress-tested in the third-generation mock data challenge.

  • GWFish

    Fisher-matrix forecasts. Extended in my M.Sc. thesis to test the nature of compact objects at ET sensitivity.

  • GWFAST

    Fisher forecasts for networks of detectors, used in the Einstein Telescope design-study work.

  • posterior-landscape

    Model-independent identification of population features and of their stability and prominence in an inferred density.
